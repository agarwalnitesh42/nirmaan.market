1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your email address (unless you contact support directly)
  • We do not collect your Aadhaar, PAN, or government ID numbers
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • We do not collect precise GPS location (district selection is manual, not GPS-based)
  • We do not collect financial information — all payment processing is handled by Razorpay

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service: Show you land opportunities in your selected districts, display khasra details, naksha images, and jamabandi records
  • Authentication: Verify your identity using phone-based login (JWT token)
  • Payments: Process property unlock purchases via Razorpay using your mobile number for checkout prefill
  • Notifications: Send push notifications about new deals, trending areas, and app updates
  • Product improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve the App experience
  • Support: Respond to your queries via email or WhatsApp

3. Data Storage & Security

3.1 Where We Store Your Data

  • Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud Platform): User accounts, preferences, saved properties, purchase records
  • Google Cloud Storage: Naksha images and property-related assets
  • Device (AsyncStorage): Authentication token, selected district, onboarding state — stored locally on your device only

3.2 How We Protect Your Data

  • All API communication uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) encryption
  • Authentication uses industry-standard JWT tokens with server-side verification
  • Payment processing is handled entirely by Razorpay, which is PCI DSS Level 1 compliant — we never see or store your card/UPI/bank details
  • Server infrastructure is hosted on Render with automatic security updates
  • Firebase services are protected by Google Cloud's security infrastructure
